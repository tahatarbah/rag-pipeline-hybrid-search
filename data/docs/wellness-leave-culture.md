# People Operations — Leave Culture Notes

Document owner: People Experience
Effective: 2026-04-01
These notes are **not** a policy. They describe how managers talk about rest. They do not replace the numbered time-off policy in the handbook.

## Taking time away

Northstar wants people to actually use their vacation. Managers should model taking a full week off, turning off Slack, and naming a coverage buddy. We talk about rest as part of the job, not a perk you have to earn twice.

When someone asks informally "how much time off do I get?", do not invent a number in Slack. Point them at the handbook paid-time-off policy. The culture notes here are about *how* to take leave, not *how many days* the bank contains.

## Vacation language we use

In all-hands we say "take the time", "use your days", "protected rest", and "coverage, not guilt". We do not quote a day count in those talks because the count lives in the numbered policy. If a new hire asks "is it unlimited vacation?", the answer is no: we ran an unlimited-PTO experiment in 2023 and rolled it back. People hoarded time off anyway, and finance could not accrue a liability.

## Manager checklist before someone goes out

1. Confirm the Workday request is approved.
2. Name a coverage owner in the team channel.
3. Pause recurring meetings.
4. Set an auto-reply that names the coverage person, not "I'll be slower".

None of this checklist changes the size of the vacation bank. It only changes whether the days actually get used.

## Common confusion

"PTO" in hallway conversation means paid time off in general. It is not a ticket number, a project code, or a facilities work order. If someone pastes a hyphenated code, open the handbook — do not guess from these culture notes.
