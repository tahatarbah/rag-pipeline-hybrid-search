## WTO-12 (Workplace Task Order)

Facilities tracking ID **WTO-12** is the lobby renovation work order for 88 Market Street. It is a construction task order, not an HR leave code. Do not file time-off questions against WTO-12 in the facilities tracker.

### Scope

WTO-12 covers:

- Demolition of the 2021 reception desk
- New stone threshold and badge readers
- Temporary plywood tunnel along the west wall (six weeks)
- After-hours freight elevator reservation

Budget owner: Workplace Ops. Vendor: Northline Interiors. Target completion: 2026-08-29.

### Access during WTO-12

While WTO-12 is open, employees enter through the loading dock between 07:00 and 19:00. Visitors must be walked from the dock. The receptionist sits in the dock vestibule until WTO-12 closes.

### Closing WTO-12

Punch-list items stay on the WTO-12 Jira epic (`FAC-WTO-12`). Facilities will not close WTO-12 until the fire marshal signs the lobby occupancy card.

If you were looking for paid time off, this document will not help. Search the People handbook instead of this work order. Do not confuse WTO-12 with the HR policy named PTO-12.
