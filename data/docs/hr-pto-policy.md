# Northstar Employee Handbook — Time Off

Document owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01
Policy ID: PTO-12

## PTO-12: Paid Time Off

Policy **PTO-12** is the canonical paid-time-off policy for Northstar full-time employees. Do not use the retired policy PTO-08 (the 15-day bank). PTO-12 replaced PTO-08 on January 1, 2026.

### Accrual

Full-time employees accrue **20 vacation days** per calendar year, granted on January 1 (not earned monthly). Unused days roll over up to a cap of 5 days. Part-time employees (under 30 hours/week) accrue PTO at 50% of the full-time bank: 10 days.

New hires in Q1 receive the full bank. New hires in Q2–Q4 receive a prorated bank: 15 days (Q2), 10 days (Q3), 5 days (Q4).

### Requesting leave

Submit requests in Workday at least 5 business days ahead for absences of 3 days or fewer, and 15 business days ahead for absences longer than 3 days. Managers must approve or decline within 3 business days. Blackout dates for Finance and Support are the last 10 business days of each fiscal quarter.

### Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO-12. Northstar provides 10 sick days per year. Sick leave does not roll over. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, People Ops may request a clinician note.

### Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive 8 weeks. Parental leave does not deduct from the PTO-12 vacation bank.

### Contact

Questions about PTO-12 go to people@northstar.example and copy your manager. Do not ask Engineering about leave balances.
