# Vendor Desk — Operational Exceptions

Document owner: Procurement
Effective: 2026-03-20
Desk: vendor-exceptions

## What counts as an exception here

The vendor desk logs shipping exceptions, invoice exceptions, and catalog exceptions. This is not the security control-exception process. Do not file a GRC ticket on this desk.

### Shipping exception

A shipping exception is a missed SLA on inbound hardware. Open a ticket with type "ShipEx". Name the carrier and the PO. The desk auto-closes shipping exceptions after the dock scan.

### Invoice exception

An invoice exception is a three-way match failure (PO, receipt, invoice). AP will not pay until the exception is cleared. Managers cannot "accept the risk" here; Procurement ops can.

### Catalog exception

A catalog exception lets a buyer purchase an item not on the approved list, up to $2,500, with a director note. Catalog exceptions expire in 30 days.

## How this differs from security

People sometimes paste "exception" into search and land here. If the question is about compensating controls or GRC Jira, leave this page. The vendor desk cannot sign a trust-services deviation. We also do not store customer data and we are out of scope for the annual audit window.
