# Northstar Benefits Guide

Document owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01
Policy ID: BEN-4

## Medical, dental, vision

Northstar pays 80% of the employee medical premium on the Gold PPO plan and 60% of dependent premiums. Coverage starts on the first of the month after your start date. Enroll in Workday within 30 days of hire or during Open Enrollment (November 1–15).

Dental and vision are 100% employer-paid for employees; dependents are employee-paid.

## 401(k)

The company match is 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay. You are eligible on day one. The match vests after 12 months of service. Catch-up contributions follow IRS limits.

## Wellness stipend

Full-time employees receive a **$750/year** wellness stipend (gym, therapy copays, meditation apps). Submit receipts in Workday under BEN-4. The stipend does not roll over. This is separate from the PTO-12 vacation bank and from sick leave.

## Employee assistance

The EAP hotline is 1-800-555-0199 (24/7). Usage is confidential and is not reported to managers.
