# Remote and hybrid work

Document owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-04-01
Policy ID: FLEX-2

## Default model

Northstar is hybrid. Employees whose role is tagged **Office-hybrid** in Workday are expected in a Northstar office **Tuesday through Thursday**. Monday and Friday are remote-optional.

Roles tagged **Remote-eligible** (mostly Engineering and Support) have no office requirement. They must overlap 10:00–16:00 in their local timezone for standups.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 in the employee’s local timezone. Meetings outside core hours need 24 hours’ notice.

## Equipment

Remote-eligible staff receive a home-office stipend of **$500 once** in the first 90 days (desk, chair, monitor). Laptops still come from IT — do not expense computers (see EXP-3).

## International remote

Working from another country for more than 15 business days in a quarter requires People Ops and Legal approval. Informal “workcations” under 15 days are allowed if your manager agrees in writing.
