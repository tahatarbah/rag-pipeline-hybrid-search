# Finance — Employee Expenses

Document owner: Finance Operations
Effective: 2026-01-15
Policy ID: EXP-3

## Expense report deadline

Submit expense reports in Expensify by the **5th business day of the following month**. Reports filed after that date land in the next cycle and may delay reimbursement by up to 30 days. The expense report deadline does not move for travel; file a partial report if receipts are still incoming.

Reimbursement is via the next payroll ACH. There is no petty cash.

## Categories and limits

- Meals while traveling: $75/day (US), $90/day (UK/EU). Alcohol is not reimbursable except for approved customer dinners, which need a VP pre-approval note.
- Hotels: cap $250/night in most US cities; $350/night in SF, NYC, London. Anything above needs a pre-trip exception from Finance.
- Laptops and peripherals: do not expense. File a request in the IT portal. The only hardware that can be expensed is replacement cables under $40.
- Conferences: ticket + travel must be in the approved learning budget. Attach the conference name; "industry event" is not a sufficient description.

## Receipts

Receipts are required above $25. A credit-card line item is not a receipt. Missing receipts over $75 will be rejected; under $75, a missing-receipt affidavit in Expensify is allowed twice per year.

## Project codes

Engineering must tag expenses to a project code (`PLAT`, `DATA`, `MOBILE`, `SEC`). Untagged reports bounce automatically. People Ops and Finance use `GNA`.
