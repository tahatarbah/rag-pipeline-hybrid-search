# Travel and booking

Document owner: Finance Operations
Effective: 2026-03-01
Policy ID: TRV-1

## Booking

Book flights and hotels in Navan. Do not book on a personal card except emergencies. The expense report deadline in EXP-3 still applies for out-of-pocket items.

## Class of travel

Flights under 6 hours: economy. Flights 6 hours or longer: premium economy is allowed. Business class requires VP Finance approval **before** booking.

## Per diem vs receipts

US domestic trips use receipts under EXP-3 (meals $75/day). International trips may use per diem published by Finance each January. You cannot mix per diem and actuals on the same day.

## Customer visits

Customer-facing dinners need a pre-approval note from a VP. Alcohol is reimbursable only on those dinners (see EXP-3).
