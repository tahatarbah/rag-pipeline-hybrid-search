# Procurement and vendors

Document owner: Finance Operations
Effective: 2026-01-20
Policy ID: PROC-2

## When you need a PO

Software or services over **$5,000/year** need a purchase order in NetSuite before the vendor is used. Card spend under $5,000 can go on a department Stripe card with manager approval.

## Security review

Vendors that receive customer data require a Security review (see the SOC 2 exception process). Do not sign a DPA yourself — Legal owns the template.

## Exceptions

The vendor exceptions desk (see vendor-exceptions.md) handles shipping and catalog exceptions. It does **not** approve SOC 2 control exceptions. Those go to the Security Director.
